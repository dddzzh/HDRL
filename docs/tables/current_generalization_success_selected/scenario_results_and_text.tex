\paragraph{Scope of the comparison.}
D3QN+SAC has the lowest mean energy in all 27 conditions of this selected panel. The relative reductions range from 18.38\% to 84.59\% against DQN-Edge, from 14.36\% to 57.02\% against DReaM-DQN, and from 10.92\% to 65.47\% against OOAC. These are descriptive comparisons without significance tests or confidence intervals. The 20 evaluation seeds do not constitute independent training runs. Changes across deadline regimes do not establish an isolated causal effect, and comparisons across resource sizes use different trained policies and resource-dependent reference deadlines. The selected-panel attainment values must not be presented as unconditional population success probabilities.
